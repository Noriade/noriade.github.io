New website LeBonbon


Customer for several years now, Le Bonbon, an online magazine and print publication distributed free of charge each month in the largest cities in France, is getting a makeover.

Few graphic changes are visible on the surface. The main evolution is in the core platform and its infrastructure.

We expect these changes to make the site more scalable, more efficient and even more stable.
